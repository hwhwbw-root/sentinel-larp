✨ How to use these elements in Canva

1

Method A: 1-Click Copy & Paste

Click "Copy SVG (Canva)" on any element below, switch to your Canva document, and press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V). It pastes as an editable vector graphic!

2

Method B: Drag & Drop Files

Drag any .svg or .png file from the canva-bunting-assets/ folder straight into Canva's Uploads panel.

3

All-in-One Master Sheet

Upload 00_SENTINEL_CANVA_MASTER_SHEET.svg to Canva. Click Ungroup to have all logos, letters, and flags instantly ready on your canvas.

4

Printing & Assembling Bunting

The pennant flags have a top fold flap and punch-hole guides. Print on 200–300gsm white cardstock, fold the top over twine/string, and hole-punch or tape.
